A structured 4-stage process to turn your vision into high-impact digital solutions.

From Idea to Execution

Design, code, testing, and launch — with a structured 4-step process, I bring your idea to life, always aiming for the best performance for your project. And as part of my commitment to your success, you get exclusive bonuses that increase your website’s visibility and impact.

Initial planning & strategic briefing

Align expectations and deeply understand your project goals.

What I do

  • Briefing meeting: I understand your needs, objectives, and brand vision to define the project goals clearly.
  • Market & competitor analysis: I study your industry and competitors to identify opportunities and differentiators.
  • Platform & tooling selection: I select the best platforms, frameworks, and technologies to ensure performance, scalability, and easy maintenance.
  • Initial visual direction: I define the color palette, typography, and visual identity, aligning the design with your brand and creating an attractive, functional experience.
  • Basic wireframe: I create an initial wireframe that defines the website structure for your approval, ensuring navigation and layout match user needs.
  • Personalized information PDF: I deliver a document with the detailed timeline, budget, selected technologies, delivery scope, and communication plan to guarantee total clarity and alignment.
  • Contract: I formalize scope, timelines, budget, and responsibilities, plus payment terms and guarantees.
Deliverables: A detailed strategy document, a basic wireframe, a personalized PDF with timeline, budget and technologies, and a formal contract.

Integrated design & development

Build a visually impactful, functional, and efficient product, integrating design and development continuously and collaboratively.

What I do

  • Personalized client area: you get access to an exclusive panel where you can customize key elements of your website, such as color palette, fonts, banners, logo, images, and products — in a simple, interactive way.
  • Agile development: I use agile methods to build iteratively, developing key features, testing, and adjusting as the project progresses.
  • Visual design & UX/UI: I focus on aesthetics and user experience, aligning the website with your brand identity.
  • Responsive design: I ensure the website adapts perfectly to different devices and screen sizes, delivering a smooth experience on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
  • Approved design ready for testing: the visual design is approved, and interface elements are ready for final implementation.
  • Continuous integration: design and development move together, ensuring a consistent experience while the code is optimized for performance and scalability.
Deliverables: A website in development with implemented features, an approved design, access to the client panel, and a detailed development roadmap.

Testing, refinements & quality assurance

Ensure the product is optimized across devices, with focus on performance, security, and user experience.

What I do

  • Usability & functionality testing: I validate all features to ensure the website is intuitive and easy to use.
  • Performance optimization: I improve loading times and overall website performance.
  • SEO & accessibility: I adjust the website to be easier to find on Google and accessible to all users.
  • Content review: I review all website content (texts, images, links) to ensure everything is correct, up to date, and error-free.
Deliverables: A detailed report with test results and applied refinements.

Launch, monitoring & ongoing maintenance

Launch with confidence, closely track performance, and continuously improve.

What I do

  • Website walkthrough & tutorial: I present the website and provide a clear tutorial so you can manage content and publish updates with confidence.
  • Social media launch kit: I deliver launch assets with images, videos, and graphic materials so you can promote your project across social networks and other channels.
  • Deployment & monitoring: I publish your website and monitor it to ensure everything runs smoothly and to detect any issues.
  • 1 month of free maintenance: I provide free maintenance during the first month to make adjustments, apply fixes, and ensure everything is working perfectly.
  • Direct WhatsApp support (basic, unlimited): you get unlimited basic support from the moment you become a client, with direct WhatsApp contact to solve any questions or issues related to the website.
Deliverables: A fully functional, production-ready website, unlimited basic WhatsApp support, a social media launch kit, and 30 days of free maintenance.

Design Online

With Design Online, you get access to an exclusive dashboard where you can fine-tune the main elements of your website based on your preferences.

  • Define the color palette: Choose your website’s main colors to match your brand’s visual identity.
  • Choose the fonts: Select fonts for headings, subheadings, and body text, creating a clear, personalized reading hierarchy.
  • Choose the banner style: Customize the main banner style (background image, slider, or video) to make the best first impression.
  • Upload your logo: Upload your logo or share guidelines so I can create something exclusive for your brand.
  • Add images and products: Send product images and other visuals, plus details like price, description, and size, so I can integrate everything directly into your website.
